Bodyscope 3D

Explore a life-size 3D human body in your own space.

Three layers to explore

Layer

Skeleton

Walk around the body’s framework and learn the names of bones, from the skull to the feet, at life size.

Layer

Muscles

Move through the muscular system and see how major muscle groups sit in relation to one another.

Layer

Organs

Look around the body’s organs in place, and tap to see each one’s name.

How it works

01

A life-size body in your room

On Apple Vision Pro, a human figure appears in the space around you, scaled to life. You can walk around it and study each layer where you are.

02

Tap a part to see its name

Select a bone, muscle, or organ and its name appears, so you can learn as you look.

Educational use only

Bodyscope 3D is for educational use only. It is not medical advice, a diagnostic tool, or a substitute for care from a qualified professional.
